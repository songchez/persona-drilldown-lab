# PDL Persona Drilldown

## Overview

Analyze one or more personas deeply against a signal. This is where surface reactions are translated into fears, desires, likely behaviors, and opportunity signals.

## When to Use

- After personas are sampled.
- When the user selects a specific persona to explore.
- When a headline needs to be translated into human behavior.

## Drilldown Fields

For each persona, produce:

1. **Reality repair** — make odd combinations plausible.
2. **Surface reaction** — what they would say first.
3. **Hidden fear** — what the event threatens.
4. **Hidden desire** — what they want but may not say.
5. **Meaning translation** — how the event becomes relevant to their life.
6. **Likely behavior** — search, discuss, buy, avoid, share, test.
7. **Search keywords** — what they may actually type.
8. **Opportunity signal** — what product/content/service concept this reaction hints at.

## Output Style

Prefer Korean, concrete, and direct. Repair strange persona combinations into realistic biographies.

## Common Pitfalls

1. Staying at generic demographic analysis.
2. Skipping behavior prediction.
3. Jumping to ideas before extracting psychological material.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Surface and hidden reactions are distinct.
- [ ] Behaviors are specific.
- [ ] Opportunity signal is stated without overbuilding a product yet.
